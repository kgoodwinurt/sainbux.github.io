## 2025-05-15 - [Smooth Scrolling & Accessibility]
**Learning:** Adding `scroll-behavior: smooth` provides a polished feel for internal navigation, but should be paired with `rel="noopener noreferrer"` for external links to ensure security. ARIA labels are essential for icon-only social links to maintain WCAG AA compliance.
**Action:** Always include ARIA labels for icon links and ensure all external links use modern security attributes.
