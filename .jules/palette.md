## 2026-06-27 - Context-Aware UX Enhancements
**Learning:** UX enhancements tailored for icon-only buttons (like `aria-label` and specific margins) can break visual consistency and cause redundancy when applied to standard text links within paragraphs.
**Action:** Always distinguish between interactive icons and inline text links when applying accessibility and styling improvements. Use semantic markers or context-specific selectors to target the correct elements.
