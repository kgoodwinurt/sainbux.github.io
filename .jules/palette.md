## 2026-07-12 - [Accessibility & Usability Hardening]
**Learning:** Combining multiple micro-UX improvements (Skip Link, accessible contrast, focus states, and descriptive labels) provides a compounding effect on usability and compliance that far outweighs individual changes.
**Action:** Always check for "Skip to Content" and WCAG contrast compliance even when the primary task is simpler, as these are low-effort high-impact "Palette" wins.
