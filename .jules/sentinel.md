## 2026-02-13 - Absence of Resource Integrity, CSP, and Reverse Tab-Nabbing Mitigations
**Vulnerability:** The codebase lacked Content Security Policy (CSP) headers/meta tags, Subresource Integrity (SRI) hashes on external CDN assets (specifically Font Awesome), and used `target="_blank"` on external links without `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
**Learning:** Without CSP and SRI, the static portfolio pages are susceptible to Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), domain hijacking, and unauthorized third-party script execution if a CDN is compromised. Further, the absence of `rel="noopener noreferrer"` on `target="_blank"` anchors permits reverse tab-nabbing, exposing users to redirection or session hijack risks.
**Prevention:** Always bundle a strict, meta-tag-based Content Security Policy that restricts resource domains, enforce Subresource Integrity (SRI) hashes on all external assets loaded from CDNs, and verify that all external links with `target="_blank"` utilize safe relations.
