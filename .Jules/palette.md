## 2026-06-08 - Brand Color Contrast Improvement
**Learning:** The initial brand color (#009fdf) failed WCAG AA contrast ratio (3.03:1) when used on white backgrounds for buttons and links. Switching to a darker shade (#007bbd) achieves a 4.6:1 ratio, meeting accessibility standards while maintaining brand identity.
**Action:** Always verify contrast ratios for brand colors used in interactive elements and prefer #007bbd for blue buttons in this design system.
