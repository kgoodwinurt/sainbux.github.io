## 2026-06-28 - Improve Accessibility and UX through Contrast and Navigation Fixes
**Learning:** Legacy button colors often fail WCAG contrast standards, and internal navigation in multi-page static sites can easily become inconsistent. Adding smooth scrolling and descriptive ARIA labels to icon-only buttons significantly improves the perceived quality and accessibility of the site.
**Action:** Always verify color contrast (aim for > 4.5:1) and ensure internal links are consistent and functional across all pages.
